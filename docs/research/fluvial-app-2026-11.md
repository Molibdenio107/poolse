## What the app is

A members' app for one club with several sports (swimming school, water polo, artistic swimming,
rowing). The store copy promises: club news, the **membership card in the app**, **access history**,
**evaluations**, the **family's conta-corrente**, and **payments in the app**. Screenshots show four
screens: a home with news and the member card (QR), a **month calendar** with filters (activities,
events, other), a **Modalidades** screen listing sports over an aquatic photo with the club's logo,
and a **Mais** menu (evaluations, account balance, access history, profile).

## Worth borrowing

1. **The member card as a screen** — a QR the desk can scan, the sócio number, the season. It is
   POOLSE-68's check-in from the family's side and costs one screen once 68 exists. Not a ticket
   until 68 is built; add it to 68's scope line.
2. **"Mais" as the fourth tab instead of "Perfil"** — Fluvial parks evaluations, balance, history
   and profile behind one *Mais*. Poolse's diamond is *Perfil*, and POOLSE-176 already moves
   settings one level down; no change, but if the family app grows past four tabs, *Mais* is the
   known shape.
3. **Calendar filters** (*aulas · eventos · fechos*) — POOLSE-175's strip could take the same
   three chips once meets (POOLSE-81) show in the app. Note for 175's follow-up, not its scope.

## Not worth borrowing

- A news feed. Avisos with a window were a deliberate call (POOLSE-166); a feed needs an editor.
- A photo background on the sport screen. POOLSE-127 uses a wash; a photo per club is content the
  office has to produce and keep.
- Access history without access control.
