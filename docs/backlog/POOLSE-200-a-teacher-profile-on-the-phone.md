# POOLSE-200 · A teacher's profile on the phone

> Part of the Poolse backlog. Conventions in [CONVENTIONS.md](./CONVENTIONS.md) apply to this ticket and are not repeated here.

**Depends on** POOLSE-197. **Priority:** Medium (pilot).

Rui: *"the teacher should have a similar profile section to the student, but with document's or settings adapted to the teacher."*

1. Name, photo, password and settings, as the student's.
2. **Qualifications:** each with its colour, read-only. **Open:** sending a new certificate from the phone. Only the office files one today, because a valid certificate decides who may teach, so it needs an office-confirms step. Default if unanswered: read-only.
3. **My details:** own address, phone, emergency contact and ID document, editable. NIF and NISS read-only.
4. **Not on the phone** (Rui): the contract and holidays, which stay on the desktop.
